# Investment memo: Project Cornerstone

**Asset:** 200-unit stabilized multifamily property
**Ask:** approve $19.7M equity for a $47M acquisition (60% LTV)

## Recommendation
Proceed. At a $47M basis (a **6.01% going-in cap**, above the
5.25% debt rate, so leverage is accretive), the deal underwrites to a **12.6% levered IRR**
and a **1.74x equity multiple** over a 5-year hold, with a minimum
**DSCR of 1.50x** and a **10.02% debt yield**. The exit is held flat to entry at a
6.00% cap, so the return is driven by NOI growth and amortization, not cap-rate compression.

## Key risks
- **Exit cap expansion** is the dominant risk; the sensitivity above isolates it against rent growth.
- **Rent growth** below 3.5% compresses returns; underwriting holds it conservative.
- **Rate / refinancing:** debt is sized to a 1.50x DSCR and 10.02% debt yield for cushion.

## Method and references
Standard real-estate underwriting: pro forma to NOI, cap-rate valuation, amortizing debt with DSCR and
debt yield, and a forward-NOI exit. Following Geltner & Miller, *Commercial Real Estate Analysis and
Investments*; NCREIF and ULI market conventions; cap-rate / NOI / DSCR as used in Argus Enterprise.
